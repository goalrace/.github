Goal Race is a race finder and training calendar for obstacle, HYROX-style hybrid and trail races.

- **Find a race.** Search by country, series, month, distance, climb, terrain or nearest airport.
- **Save it.** Keep your races on one list, plus any race we do not list.
- **Plan the training.** Put a full training plan on your calendar, every session dated, ending on race week.
- **Log the week.** Mark sessions done, keep notes, review any week or month.

## Use it from Claude

Goal Race is also an MCP connector. Add it to Claude and ask in plain words:
*"Find a Spartan Beast in Germany in May"*, *"Put a 12-week plan on my calendar"*, *"What's on today?"*
